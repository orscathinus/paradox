The Modern Era

The age in which the state became mass, industrial, ideological and global. Revolutions mobilized populations, railways and factories remade power, empires fractured into nations, and technologies steadily collapsed the distance between decisions and consequences. Paradox Atlas treats this layer broadly so that a campaign can later descend into the Napoleonic age, industrial century, world wars, Cold War or the contemporary system.
