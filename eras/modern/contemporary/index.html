The Contemporary Age

The Cold War ended without ending history. The decades after 1991 expanded trade, digital networks and international institutions while also producing new wars, new inequalities, new forms of nationalism and eventually a renewed contest among major powers. By the 2020s, economic security, chips, energy routes, satellites, supply chains and information systems have become strategic terrain alongside armies and borders.
