2026

This layer treats 2026 as contemporaries experience it: not as a chapter with a known ending, but as a field of active pressures, incomplete information and plausible alternatives. Economic forecasts may be revised; governments bargain; crises can widen or recede; technology programmes can succeed, slip or fail. The flavor system therefore separates historical anchors from dynamic gameplay events.
