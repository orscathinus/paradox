Region of the World · 2026

South Asia

The Himalaya and Indian Ocean frame one of the most densely inhabited political spaces on Earth. Version 1.0.3 makes that neighbourhood much more visible inside India’s playable tree: Bangladesh, Nepal, the Maldives, Sri Lanka and Pakistan now appear across radically different democratic, communist, fascist and imperial futures, while China increasingly shapes the northern strategic horizon. These are alternate-history mechanics; each neighbouring state remains reserved for its own independent country module.

Regional Design Principle

Version 1.0.3 deliberately lets India’s ideologies imagine different regional orders, but those branches are still written from India’s campaign perspective. When Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Sri Lanka or the Maldives become playable, their own focus trees will be able to accept, resist, exploit or overturn those Indian ambitions rather than existing as passive map objects.
