South Asia

The Himalaya and Indian Ocean frame one of the most densely inhabited strategic spaces on Earth. Continental security, river systems, migration, nuclear deterrence, trade corridors, energy imports and political traditions cross borders that remain intensely consequential. Version 1.0.0 begins here because India alone contains enough internal variation to demonstrate the archive's full depth without treating its neighbors as footnotes.

Regional Design Principle

Neighboring states will not exist merely as props for India's focus tree. When activated, each receives its own statistics, national spirits, focus branches, event bank and lore dossier; shared disputes and partnerships can then connect separate national systems rather than collapsing one country's perspective into another's.
