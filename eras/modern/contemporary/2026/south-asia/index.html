Regional design principle

Alternate Indian focus branches may pressure, court, associate with or fight neighbouring states, but those scenarios do not replace the neighbours’ own future modules. When Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Sri Lanka or the Maldives become playable, their domestic politics and strategic choices will be written from their own point of view.
