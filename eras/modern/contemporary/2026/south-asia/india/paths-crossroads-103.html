Version 1.0.3 · National Root

A Republic Already in Motion

The tree does not begin from emptiness. India enters 2026 with institutions, infrastructure, rivalries, social tensions, global partnerships, space programs and development plans already underway. The opening focuses are therefore acts of interpretation: before the player chooses an ideology or industrial doctrine, the state must decide what it thinks its present means.

National Root

☸ India at the Crossroads

The government receives a country that is simultaneously richer and poorer than simple labels suggest: a nuclear power with district hospitals still under strain, a spacefaring state with cities still learning how to drain monsoon rain, a giant domestic market whose firms compete across radically unequal regions. The first focus is not a policy. It is a national inventory.

Every ministry produces a different map of the future. Industrial planners see freight corridors and machine tools. Social ministries see schools, jobs and urban migration. Diplomats see China, the Indian Ocean and a fragmented world system. Political leaders see the unresolved question of whether India’s diversity is a source of resilience or an obstacle to speed.

Version 1.0.3 begins by making those contradictions playable rather than smoothing them into one progress bar. India is powerful enough to choose among futures, but not powerful enough to pursue every future at the same time.

Gameplay: unlocks the political crossroads, parallel economic tree, shared state/society branches and science base.
Political Choice Root

⚖ The Constitutional Question

As the country’s strategic ambitions rise, an older question returns in a new form: what kind of state can carry them? One camp argues the Constitution’s federal democracy is precisely what makes India durable. Another believes class revolution is required to unlock the country’s human scale. A fascist movement demands centralized national will. Restorationists argue that a crown can embody continuity that party politics cannot.

These are not four skins over the same mechanics. Each path changes how legitimacy is generated, how states relate to the center, how dissent is treated, how neighbours interpret Indian power and what kinds of expansion become politically possible.

The focus marks the last moment when all roads remain open. Once India chooses a political future, the other three do not wait politely in reserve. Their organizations become opposition, underground movements, émigré networks or memories of the road not taken.

Gameplay: enables four mutually exclusive ideological gates. Economic, space and shared national branches remain separately playable regardless of the choice.
