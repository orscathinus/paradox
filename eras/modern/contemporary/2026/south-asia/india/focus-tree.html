A Republic of Branching Choices

India does not develop in six clean vertical columns. Federal bargaining changes infrastructure; education changes industrial depth; ports change diplomacy; climate changes cities; space policy depends on research institutions and patient capital. Version 1.0.1 rebuilds the tree around those entanglements. Lines are real prerequisites, dashed lines mark cross-branch dependencies, and every focus now leads to a long-form dossier rather than a one-paragraph tooltip.

How to read the tree: solid connectors are prerequisites inside a policy family; dashed connectors cross policy families. Several late-game focuses deliberately require progress in two or three branches. The climate line depends on industrial and social capacity. Maritime policy depends on diplomacy and corridors. National institutional resilience depends on science, productivity and external strategy. This is intentional: the country is a system, not a set of six unrelated minigames.
