India

A civilization-state, a federal republic, a coalition government and an aspiring great power moving beneath the same tricolor. In 2026 India carries a central paradox of the century: world-class capability exists beside immense unfinished development, and both are real. The campaign begins not with a blank slate, but with a Republic already in motion. Jai Hind.

National Spirits · Gameplay

The Republic begins with momentum—and friction.

The 2047 Horizon

The centenary of independence turns “developed India” from a distant aspiration into a deadline that ministries, companies and voters can invoke.

Coalition Arithmetic

The third Modi government remains powerful, but the parliamentary landscape requires greater attention to allies and regional bargaining than the previous two terms.

Strategic Autonomy

India cooperates widely while resisting the idea that partnership must become alignment under another power's strategy.

Aatmanirbhar Imperative

Defence, electronics and critical technologies are increasingly measured by whether domestic ecosystems can absorb, design and produce them.

The Demographic Challenge

A vast working-age population can compound growth only if education, health, skills, housing and formal employment convert scale into productivity.

Monsoon Republic

Heat, water, rainfall, Himalayan systems and vulnerable coasts force climate adaptation into the everyday mechanics of development.
