The focus tree is still the map. The dossiers are now the country.

Node spacing has been tightened, prerequisite lines have been simplified, secondary dependencies are hidden unless requested, and every one of the 143 focuses now opens a page-length narrative. The short text on the tree exists only to help you navigate; it is no longer expected to carry the political, cultural or institutional weight of the choice.
