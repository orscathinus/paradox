India

A continental federal republic, a civilization of many languages and faiths, a giant consumer market, an emerging industrial power and a country attempting to translate scale into capability. Version 1.0.2 adds something the previous Atlas lacked: India is no longer assumed to follow one future. Its focus tree can preserve the constitutional republic, overturn it from the left, harden into a fascist national state, experiment with monarchy, or choose radically different economic and space doctrines. Jai Hind.
