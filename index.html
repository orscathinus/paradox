Campaign Systems

What changed.

Focus Tree

Clean pan & zoom

Drag to pan, zoom under the cursor, fit the whole tree, focus on one branch, or hide prerequisite lines. Orthogonal routing replaces much of the old spaghetti.

Ideology

Four incompatible states

Constitutional democracy, revolutionary communism, fascist national state and monarchist restoration are explicit mutual exclusions.

Economy

Three industrial Indias

Export-manufacturing powerhouse, digital-services superpower or domestic-market federation. You cannot maximize all three at once.

Space

What is space for?

Human spaceflight, robotic science or commercial orbit become incompatible priority doctrines with different long-run payoffs.

Demographics

Religion, language, class

Pie charts expose the composition beneath the national total, with the Census 2011 and PRICE 2020–21 source vintages visible.

Region

Flags across South Asia

The regional layer now gives each visible country its own actual flag and preserves them as future independent country modules.
