Version 1.0.3

The focus tree stopped behaving like a template.

Branch lengths are intentionally unequal. The fascist branch is largest and most expansion-heavy, but it spends much of its length constructing the regime itself. Democratic India can build confederations; communist India can form a revolutionary South Asian federation; imperial India can create protectorates, mandates and an Asian Concert. Economy now sits beside politics and runs at the same time.

Featured System · Rebuilt Focus Canvas

143 branches of possibility

The canvas is deliberately sprawling but navigable: cursor-centered zoom, drag-pan, quick jumps, category filters, faint secondary prerequisites and branch-local orthogonal routing keep the structure readable without flattening it into neat columns.

25-focus Fascist Route

Regime-building, corporatism, mass mobilization and cultural centralization precede a wide expansion fan across South Asia and the China rivalry.

24-focus Imperial Route

Constitutional crown or executive empire, Nepal, Bengal, Sri Lanka, the Maldives, eastern corridors and a challenge to China.

Parallel Economy

Four industrial Indias evolve beside the political struggle rather than waiting beneath it.

Political Futures

Four Indias that do not converge.

Democratic / Federal

The Republic, chosen again

Coalition government, multilingual citizenship, a South Asian Council, Himalayan compact, Bengal partnership and voluntary accession/confederation mechanics.

Communist

The Red Republic

Councils, Red Federalism, land reform, planning, revolutionary influence in Bangladesh and Nepal, a South Asian People’s Federation and rivalry with Beijing.

Fascist / Ultranationalist

The National State

The longest political route: domestic authoritarian transformation first, then protectorates, coercive hierarchy, territorial revision and continental rivalry.

Imperial / Restorationist

The Empire imagined again

A constitutional crown or executive empire branches through princely politics, oceanic monarchy, Nepal, Bengal and the struggle for Asian balance.

Parallel Systems

The country keeps developing while politics happens.

Economy, society, climate and space no longer pretend that a government must finish its ideology tree before building ports, schools or satellites.
